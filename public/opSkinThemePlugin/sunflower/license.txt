The background photograph this theme was made with (img/bg.jpg, a ぱくたそ photo:
https://www.pakutaso.com) is not included: ぱくたそ allows free redistribution only at its
S size, which is too small for a page background. To wear the theme with a photograph, pick
one whose terms allow your use, agree to those terms yourself, and place it at img/bg.jpg
beside this file; main.css already points there. Without the file the theme shows its
background colour.
